# Training and competence policy

Nobody works on [Kelvara Rhythm](/products/kelvara-rhythm.md) or with patient data before their training
is recorded as complete.[^s1]

## Required training

| Role | Initial training | Refresher |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cardiac technician | 40 hours of ECG triage training, then 2 weeks supervised at Elmhaven | Annual recertification on a test set of 200 recordings, pass mark 95% |
| Support engineer | Patient-data access rules and break-glass procedure | Every year |
| Everyone | GDPR and patient-data basics; the [incident and vigilance playbook](/playbooks/incident-vigilance.md) (first alert) | Every year |

A cardiac technician who fails the annual recertification stops triage until they pass a retest.[^s1]

## Records

- Training records are kept for the duration of employment plus 10 years.[^s1]
- The [Elmhaven monitoring centre](/sites/elmhaven-monitoring-centre.md) schedule only shows technicians
  whose certification is current.[^s1]
- Changes to the [patient-data access policy](/policies/patient-data-access.md) trigger a refresher for
  the roles concerned within 30 days.[^s1]
