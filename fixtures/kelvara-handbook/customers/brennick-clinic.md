# Brennick Cardiology Clinic

Brennick Cardiology Clinic (fictional) is a private clinic that has used
[Kelvara Rhythm](/products/kelvara-rhythm.md) since March 2026. It does not use Kelvara Suivi: its
patients get paper wear instructions.[^s1]

## Contract

- Services agreement for 2 years, until 28 February 2028.[^s1]
- About 60 exams a month. Its recordings are reviewed at the
  [Thornfield backup centre](/sites/thornfield-backup-centre.md) on weekdays, to balance the load with
  Elmhaven; urgent alerts still go to Elmhaven or the clinic's on-call cardiologist.[^s1]

## Data protection

- Brennick is the controller; Kelvara is its processor.[^s1]
- Its DPA keeps Kelvara's standard retention period, as set in the
  [data retention policy](/policies/data-retention.md).[^s1]

## Contacts (roles)

- Medical director: clinical owner of the service and the clinic's CLMV.
- Clinic DPO: an external consultant, named in the DPA.
