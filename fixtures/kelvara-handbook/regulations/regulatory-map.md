# Regulatory map

Kelvara sells a class IIa software medical device, [Kelvara Rhythm](/products/kelvara-rhythm.md), and a
patient app that is not a medical device, [Kelvara Suivi](/products/kelvara-suivi.md), to French
hospitals. The rules below apply. Each page summarises the official text for internal use; the
official text always wins.[^s1]

| Area | Rule | Page | Internal owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Device conformity | EU MDR 2017/745, EUDAMED | [MDR transition and EUDAMED](mdr-eudamed.md) | PRRC |
| Vigilance in France | ANSM matériovigilance | [ANSM matériovigilance](/regulations/ansm-materiovigilance.md) | Vigilance officer |
| Hosting health data | HDS certification | [HDS certification](hds-certification.md) | Head of IT operations |
| Reimbursement | Remote monitoring (LATM), PECAN | [Remote monitoring reimbursement](/regulations/telesurveillance-latm.md), [PECAN](pecan.md) | Market access lead |
| Interoperability and security | ANS DMN référentiel, Ségur building blocks | [Ségur du numérique](/regulations/segur-numerique.md) | Head of engineering |
| Patient identity | INS | [National health identity (INS)](ins.md) | Head of engineering |
| Personal data | GDPR, CNIL health référentiels | [CNIL health référentiels](/regulations/cnil-sante.md) | DPO |
| EU health data | EHDS, Regulation (EU) 2025/327 | [European Health Data Space](ehds.md) | DPO |

The [PRRC](/people/prrc.md) chairs a quarterly regulatory watch and updates the `verified` date on
each page after checking it against the official text.[^s1]

[^s1]: Regulatory watch, November 2026, item 1.
