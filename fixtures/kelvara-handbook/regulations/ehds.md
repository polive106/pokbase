# European Health Data Space (EHDS)

*Draft page: the application dates specific to electronic health record (EHR) systems are still to be
confirmed against Article 105 of the regulation.*

Regulation (EU) 2025/327 creates the European Health Data Space. Published in the Official Journal on
5 March 2025, it **entered into force on 26 March 2025**.[^s1]

## Timeline

- **General application: 26 March 2027.**[^s1]
- Staged application after that, with milestones on 26 March 2029, 26 March 2031 and 26 March 2035;
  **secondary use** of data (research, innovation, public policy) applies mainly from 2029.[^s1]
- Obligations for manufacturers of EHR systems apply in stages; secondary sources disagree on the exact
  dates.

## Open questions for Kelvara

- Kelvara Rhythm is a medical device; if it claims interoperability with EHR systems, it may fall under
  the requirements for EHR software components. The [DPO](/people/dpo.md) and the
  [PRRC](/people/prrc.md) must decide before 26 March 2027.
- Secondary use might one day give access to data useful for the clinical evaluation, through the
  health data access bodies.

[^s1]: Regulation (EU) 2025/327, Articles 105 and following.
