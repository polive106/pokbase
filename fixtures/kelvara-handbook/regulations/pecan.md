# PECAN (prise en charge anticipée numérique)

The PECAN is a transitional, flat-rate reimbursement for a digital medical device presumed innovative,
set up by article L. 162-1-23 of the Code de la sécurité sociale and decree n° 2023-232 of
30 March 2023. The HAS assesses the application.[^s1]

## Key rules

- It lasts **one year and cannot be renewed**.[^s1]
- During that year the manufacturer must file the full application: within **9 months** of the PECAN
  decision for a remote-monitoring device (LATM listing), within **6 months** for a therapeutic device
  (LPPR listing).[^s1]
- The first remote-monitoring device under PECAN was covered from 23 September 2024 (arrêté of
  10 September 2024).[^s2]

## Kelvara's plan

- The market-access lead prepares a PECAN application for [Kelvara Rhythm](/products/kelvara-rhythm.md),
  using the 2025 clinical evaluation and the data from the
  [post-market surveillance plan](/plans/post-market-surveillance.md).
- Because the LATM application is due 9 months after the decision and needs the ANS certificate (see
  [remote monitoring reimbursement](telesurveillance-latm.md)), the ANS certification work starts
  before the PECAN application is filed.
- Industry bodies consider one year too short; Kelvara plans as if no extension will be granted.
