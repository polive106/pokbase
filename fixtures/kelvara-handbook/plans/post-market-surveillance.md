# Post-market surveillance plan

The MDR requires a post-market surveillance (PMS) system proportionate to the device's risk class.
This plan sets it out for [Kelvara Rhythm](/products/kelvara-rhythm.md); the [PRRC](/people/prrc.md)
owns it.[^s1]

## Data sources

- Complaints, from the [complaint handling procedure](/policies/complaint-handling.md).[^s1]
- Incidents and trends, from the [incident and vigilance playbook](/playbooks/incident-vigilance.md).[^s1]
- Technician re-reviews: 2% of signed reports are re-read every month by a second technician.[^s1]
- Literature and similar devices, reviewed every 6 months.[^s1]

## Indicators and thresholds

| Indicator | Alert threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Complaints alleging a missed AF episode | More than 1 per 1,000 exams in a month |
| Disagreement on the monthly 2% re-review | More than 3% of re-read reports |
| Urgent alerts acknowledged late (over 15 minutes) | More than 0.5% in a month |

Crossing a threshold opens a CAPA, and a statistically significant increase is assessed for a trend
report.[^s1]

## Reports

- **Periodic safety update report (PSUR):** Kelvara Rhythm is class IIa, so the PSUR is updated when
  necessary and **at least every 2 years**; the next one is due in June 2027.[^s1]
- **Post-market clinical follow-up (PMCF):** a registry study at
  [Valmoor University Hospital](/customers/valmoor-university-hospital.md) compares Rhythm's flags with
  the cardiologists' final diagnoses on 500 exams a year.[^s1]
