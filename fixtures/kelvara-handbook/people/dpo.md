# Data protection officer (DPO)

The DPO is a role, not a named person. Kelvara processes health data at scale for hospitals, so it has
appointed a DPO under GDPR Article 37. The DPO reports to the CEO and is not instructed on how to
carry out the role.[^s1]

## Responsibilities

- Owns the [data retention policy](/policies/data-retention.md) and the
  [patient-data access policy](/policies/patient-data-access.md), and reviews access logs every month.[^s1]
- Decides whether a personal data breach must be notified, and is the only person who contacts a
  supervisory authority (see the [incident and vigilance playbook](/playbooks/incident-vigilance.md)).[^s1]
- Runs the [data subject requests](/playbooks/data-subject-requests.md) process.[^s1]
- Reviews every new subprocessor (with the [supplier management procedure](/policies/supplier-management.md))
  and keeps the subprocessor register.[^s1]
- Owns the DPIA for Kelvara Rhythm and Kelvara Suivi, updated at least once a year and before any
  significant change in processing.[^s1]
- Tracks the CNIL frameworks and the EHDS (see [CNIL health référentiels](/regulations/cnil-sante.md)
  and [European Health Data Space](/regulations/ehds.md)).
