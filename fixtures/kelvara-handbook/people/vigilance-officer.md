# Vigilance officer

The vigilance officer is a role in the quality team, not a named person. Two people share it on a
weekly rota, so one of them is always on duty during working hours.[^s1]

## Responsibilities

- Triages every complaint received under the [complaint handling procedure](/policies/complaint-handling.md)
  and decides, within 1 working day, whether it needs a vigilance assessment.[^s1]
- Owns incident tickets from triage to closure and drafts the MIR, follow-up and final reports for the
  [PRRC](/people/prrc.md) to sign (see the [incident and vigilance playbook](/playbooks/incident-vigilance.md)).[^s1]
- Keeps the CLMV contact of every customer hospital (see [ANSM matériovigilance](/regulations/ansm-materiovigilance.md)).[^s1]
- Prepares the monthly trend figures for the [post-market surveillance plan](/plans/post-market-surveillance.md).[^s1]

The vigilance officer does not decide reportability: that decision belongs to the PRRC.
