# Procurement note: new messaging subprocessor

From: procurement desk, Kelvara Health — 2026-10-20

We have signed a services agreement and a data processing agreement with **Wrenfield Messaging**, an
HDS-certified SMS and push-notification gateway with data centres in France.

- First use: 2 February 2027, for Kelvara Suivi reminders to patients.
- Lead time for a new sender ID: 15 working days.
- Data shared: the patient's phone number and the hospital's exam number only; no clinical data.
- Wrenfield Messaging replaces Ardoise Cloud's built-in SMS relay for Kelvara Suivi reminders.
  Ardoise Cloud keeps hosting and backups.
- Hospitals must be told about the new subprocessor at least 30 days before first use (DPA clause
  7.2), so the notices go out by 2 January 2027.
